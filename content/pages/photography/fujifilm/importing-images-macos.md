## Using a Phone

> All methods of importing to iOS really only support JPEG imports and not RAW. This is a better method than the Fujifilm app anyway though

1. On the camera, ensure that `Setup > Connection Settings > USB Mode` is set to `USB Card Reader`
2. On iOS open your `Photos` app and there should be a new `Import` section at the bottom of the screen. On Android you should be able to see your normal file explorer

## Using a Computer

Since I always forget how this works and it depends on some camera settings as well as Mac stuff, here's a general breakdown on how to do it:

1. On the camera, ensure that `Setup > Connection Settings > USB Mode` is set to `USB Card Reader`
2. Open the `Image Capture` app on Mac, this is preinstalled.
3. Plug the camera in and then turn it on, the camera should now show up in the `Image Capture` app
